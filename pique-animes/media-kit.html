Who we want to work with

Relevant brands

  • Anime and pop-culture merchandise shops
  • Comic, gaming and entertainment stores
  • Conventions, screenings and fan events
  • Independent artists and creative studios

What makes a good fit

  • Products and services suitable for a general audience
  • Clear business identity and honest claims
  • Permission to use supplied logos, images and copy
  • Campaign goals and budget that can be discussed openly

How a partnership works

1. Make an enquiry. Tell us your brand, campaign goal, preferred dates and budget range.
2. Review the fit. We assess relevance, content suitability, rights and available placements.
3. Agree in writing. Confirm deliverables, price, payment terms, schedule, creative approvals and cancellation terms before work begins.
4. Publish transparently. Paid placements are labelled as sponsored or advertising. Any reporting is limited to data actually available.
Important transparency note
Pique Animes is growing its platform. We do not currently claim a particular monthly audience, traffic volume, conversion rate or guaranteed campaign result. Pricing and availability are confirmed individually. We will not sell fake engagement, guarantee sales, disguise advertising as independent editorial, or accept campaigns that violate platform rules. Third-party anime characters, posters, footage and trademarks belong to their respective rights holders; sponsors must provide assets they are authorised to use.
