Partnership outreach toolkit

LET'S FIND THE RIGHT PARTNERS.

A practical guide for approaching relevant brands professionally. Use the templates as a starting point, personalise every message, and only promise deliverables Pique Animes can actually provide.

View our media kit ↗Contact Pique ↗

Who to approach

Start with businesses whose products genuinely fit anime fans: anime merchandise shops, comic and gaming stores, local conventions, cosplay photographers, independent artists, event organisers and entertainment retailers.

What to offer

Discuss clearly labelled sponsored placements, original sponsored articles, creator interviews, event promotion or a tailored campaign. Confirm scope, dates, price and reporting before accepting payment.

First contact email

Subject: Partnership idea with Pique Animes Hello [Name], I'm reaching out from Pique Animes, an anime discovery and fan community platform. We are exploring a small number of relevant partnerships with brands and creators. I thought [Brand] could be a good fit because [personalised reason]. We would be happy to discuss a clearly labelled sponsored feature or promotional placement, subject to availability and agreed campaign details. Our partnership overview is here: https://pique-animes.pages.dev/media-kit.html Would you be open to discussing a possible collaboration? Best, Pique Animes WhatsApp: +233 597 417 776

Follow-up message

Hello [Name], I wanted to follow up on my earlier message about a possible Pique Animes partnership. If this is relevant to your current marketing plans, I'd be glad to share more details. If not, no worries. Thank you for your time.

Before you contact a brand

Check the business is real, use its official contact channel, identify one specific reason for the fit, include the media kit, and record when you contacted them. Avoid mass spam and never pay an alleged sponsor to unlock a deal.

Honest representation matters. Pique Animes is building its audience. Do not invent traffic figures, follower counts, engagement statistics, past clients or guaranteed sales. If a brand asks for analytics, share only current figures that can be verified. Label paid content clearly and use only images, logos and material you have permission to publish.
